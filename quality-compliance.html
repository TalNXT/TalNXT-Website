Fitment before submission
AI-enabled fitment

We confirm the fit before you see the resume.

Our AI-enabled process looks past keywords to the skills, experience depth and practical ability a role actually needs. People then validate every recommendation before a profile is shared.

  1. JD decomposition

    The requirement is broken into must-have skills, good-to-haves, experience depth, domain and soft-skill expectations.

  2. AI skill matching

    Semantic matching across our active talent pool and the market ranks candidates by real skill fit, not keyword density.

  3. AI-assisted screening

    Structured technical questions confirm hands-on depth on each must-have skill.

  4. Practical assessment

    Role-relevant coding, configuration or scenario exercises prove what the candidate can actually do.

  5. Recruiter + SME validation

    A specialist recruiter and a technology SME review communication, stability and intent.

  6. Top 3 shortlist

    Only the three strongest profiles are shared, each with a fitment scorecard.

Background verification

Verified before we process, not after they join.

Background checks start as soon as a candidate is shortlisted and are cleared before onboarding. You are protected from fake credentials, undisclosed employment and compliance risk from the start.

  • Identity & PAN / Aadhaar validation
  • Education & certification verification
  • Previous employment & tenure checks
  • Address verification
  • Criminal & court record checks
  • Professional reference checks
  • Dual-employment / moonlighting check (UAN)
  • Client-specific checks on request
