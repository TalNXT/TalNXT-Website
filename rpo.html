RPO Solutions

Outsource Your Entire Recruitment - Full or Project-Based

Choose the RPO model that fits your needs. Whether you need ongoing support or help with a specific project, we've got you covered.

Full RPO

Dedicated RPO team that becomes an extension of your HR department. We handle all recruitment activities—sourcing, screening, interviewing, offer management, and onboarding coordination—on an ongoing basis.

  • Dedicated recruiters assigned to your account
  • Complete ownership of your hiring pipeline
  • Scalable team that grows with your needs
  • Ongoing partnership with regular reporting

Project-Based RPO

Perfect for time-bound hiring initiatives, new office openings, or scaling specific teams. We deploy a focused RPO team to deliver on your project requirements within the defined timeline.

  • Focused team for specific hiring targets
  • Rapid deployment within days
  • Flexible engagement terms
  • End-to-end support until project completion
Complete Process

End-to-End Support Till Onboarding

We take complete ownership of your recruitment process from requisition to candidate onboarding, ensuring seamless experience for both you and candidates.

1
Requisition & Planning

We understand your requirements, create detailed job descriptions, and develop recruitment strategies tailored to each role.

2
Sourcing & Screening

AI-powered candidate sourcing across multiple channels, followed by comprehensive screening and initial assessments by our recruiters.

3
Interview Coordination

We schedule all interviews, coordinate with hiring managers and interview panels, and ensure smooth communication throughout.

4
Offer Management

We handle offer negotiations, background checks, reference verifications, and all pre-joining formalities on your behalf.

5
Onboarding Support

We coordinate with your HR team to ensure smooth onboarding, document collection, and provide candidate handover for seamless integration.
