Our Model

Three-Phase BOT Model

Our Build-Operate-Transfer approach ensures you get a fully functional, optimized HR infrastructure that your team can seamlessly take over and manage independently.

1

BUILD Phase

We design and build your complete HR infrastructure from the ground up—policies, processes, systems, and team structure tailored to your organizational needs.

  • HR Strategy & Framework Design
  • Process & Policy Development
  • HRIS Implementation & Configuration
  • Team Structure & Role Definition
  • Compliance Framework Setup
2

OPERATE Phase

We operate your HR infrastructure, optimize processes, train your team, and refine systems based on real-world performance and organizational feedback.

  • Day-to-Day HR Operations
  • Process Optimization & Refinement
  • Team Training & Development
  • Performance Monitoring & Reporting
  • Continuous Improvement
3

TRANSFER Phase

Complete knowledge transfer, documentation handover, and transition support to ensure your team can seamlessly take over and operate independently.

  • Complete Documentation Handover
  • Knowledge Transfer Sessions
  • Transition Support & Guidance
  • Post-Transfer Support Period
  • Independent Operation Readiness
Benefits

Why Choose BOT Model?

Build-Operate-Transfer model provides you with proven HR infrastructure, operational expertise, and complete ownership without long-term dependency.

Proven Infrastructure from Day One

Get a battle-tested HR infrastructure built with best practices, avoiding trial-and-error and costly mistakes.

Learn from Operations

Your team learns while we operate, gaining hands-on experience and insights before taking full ownership.

Complete Ownership & Independence

Own your HR infrastructure completely after transfer, with full control and no long-term dependency on external vendors.

Cost-Effective Solution

Lower total cost of ownership compared to building internally or long-term outsourcing, with clear ROI and predictable costs.
