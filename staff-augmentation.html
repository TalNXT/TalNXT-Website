Engagement models

Flexible models that fit how you deliver.

Choose the model that matches your project horizon and hiring plans. Every model includes the same screening, compliance and account management.

Time & Material Contractors

Skilled professionals on TalNXT's payroll, working as part of your team for as long as you need them. Monthly billing on approved timesheets.

  • Individual specialists or multiple hires
  • 3–36 month assignments with easy extensions
  • Statutory compliance fully managed by TalNXT
Contract-to-Hire

Evaluate a resource on live work before making a permanent offer. Convert at an agreed point with transparent, pre-agreed conversion terms.

  • Lower hiring risk for critical roles
  • Conversion after a pre-agreed tenure
  • Smooth transfer of records and assets
Project-Based Ramp Teams

Ramp several resources together for a programme, migration or release, then scale down as milestones close.

  • 10+ resources ramped in 2–3 weeks
  • Skill mix planned against your delivery plan
  • Staggered release aligned to milestones
End-to-end resource lifecycle

One partner from requirement to renewal.

Every stage is owned by TalNXT and tracked by your dedicated account manager, so nothing falls between the gaps.

  1. Requirement

    We break the JD down into must-have skills, experience depth, domain and practical capabilities with your hiring manager.

  2. AI-Enabled Sourcing

    AI matching across our active talent pool and the market finds candidates who fit the role, not just the keywords.

  3. Fitment Validation

    Technical screening, a practical assessment and recruiter and SME review. Only the top 3 profiles are shared.

  4. Client Interview

    We coordinate the interviews, collect feedback within 24 hrs and manage offers and notice periods.

  5. BGV & Onboarding

    Background verification, compliance training, documentation and day-one readiness, done before joining.

  6. Deployment

    Laptop, access and client induction are coordinated, and the resource is productive from week one.

  7. Performance Tracking

    Monthly client feedback, resource health check-ins and quarterly reviews, with early warning on any risk.

  8. Renewal / Extension / Release

    Proactive renewal planning, extensions or a structured knowledge-transfer and release.
