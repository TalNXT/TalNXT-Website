Flexible engagement options

Our Engagement Models

The right talent. The right engagement. Greater workforce flexibility.

C2C – Contract-to-Contract

On-demand specialists for immediate project and delivery needs, engaged on TalNXT's contract and billed monthly on approved timesheets.

  • Individual specialists or full pods
  • Short notice starts for urgent gaps
  • Extend, swap or release as plans change
C2H – Contract-to-Hire

Evaluate talent on live work before making a permanent offer, then convert the right fit on transparent, pre-agreed terms.

  • Lower hiring risk for critical roles
  • Conversion after a pre-agreed tenure
  • Smooth transfer of records and assets
Project-Based Contingent Teams

Ramp several resources together for a programme, migration or release, then scale down as milestones close.

  • 10+ resources ramped in 2–3 weeks
  • Skill mix planned against your delivery plan
  • Staggered release aligned to milestones
When contingent staffing fits

Capacity for the moments permanent hiring can't cover.

Contingent talent lets you respond to real demand quickly, without adding long-term cost or slowing delivery down.

Urgent delivery gaps

Fill a critical skill gap in days when a release, client commitment or go-live date can't move.

Peak and seasonal demand

Add capacity for busy periods, large migrations or new programmes and release it once demand settles.

Niche or short-term skills

Bring in specialists for a defined scope, such as a cloud migration or security audit, without a permanent hire.

Headcount freezes

Keep projects moving when permanent headcount is on hold, with costs that sit outside fixed payroll.

Leave and backfill cover

Cover parental leave, sabbaticals or attrition while you plan the long-term hire.
