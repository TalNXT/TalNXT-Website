Workforce Operations & Employee Experience

Beyond Deployment. Complete Workforce Management.

We take care of the operational and employee lifecycle responsibilities that keep your contingent workforce compliant, engaged, productive and supported.

  • Trusted & Verified Talent

    Background verification, identity and employment checks, credential validation and compliance readiness before deployment.

  • Structured Onboarding

    Documentation, joining formalities, policy orientation and smooth onboarding for deployment readiness.

  • Payroll, Statutory & Compliance

    Timely payroll, PF, ESI, PT, TDS and applicable labour-law compliance, supported by accurate records and documentation.

    Monthly timesheet tracking, approval coordination, consolidated invoicing and workforce administration.

    Employee connect, grievance support, rewards and recognition initiatives, and retention-focused engagement practices.
