Privacy Policy

How TalNXT Technologies collects, uses and protects the personal data you share with us through this website, and the choices and rights you have.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by TalNXT Technologies Private Limited (“TalNXT”, “we”, “us”), 2nd & 3rd Floor, Balaji Arcade, 427/7, 20th Main, Koramangala VI Block, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560095, India. For the personal data described here, TalNXT is the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).

2. What we collect

  • Business enquiries: your name, company, work email, phone number, and the details you give about your staffing or HR requirement.
  • Job applications and resumes: your name, email, phone number, current location, experience, notice period, LinkedIn profile link, any message you add, the role you are applying for, and your resume (PDF or DOCX).
  • Emails and calls: what you share when you contact us directly.
  • Security data: when you submit a form, your IP address and browser signals are processed by Cloudflare to block spam and abuse (Cloudflare Turnstile and rate limiting). We do not store your IP address with your submission.
  • Website analytics: only if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site (pages visited, device and approximate location). See section 9.

Please do not include sensitive information (such as financial, health or identity document details) in forms or resumes. We do not need it at this stage.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss our services with you.
  • To assess your profile for the role you applied for and for similar current or future roles, and to contact you about them.
  • To send you a confirmation that we received your submission.
  • To keep the website and our systems secure and prevent misuse.
  • To understand and improve how the website is used (analytics, only with your consent).
  • To meet legal obligations and to establish or defend legal claims.

4. Our basis for processing

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you tick the consent box on our forms or accept analytics cookies, and, where the DPDP Act allows, for legitimate uses such as responding to a request you have made to us or complying with the law. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 7). Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf: Microsoft (Microsoft 365, where submissions, resumes and emails are stored), Cloudflare (which receives form submissions and provides spam protection) and Google (analytics, only with your consent).
  • Prospective employers (candidates only): we share your profile with a client only for roles you have agreed to be considered for.
  • Authorities: where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Some of these providers may process data outside India. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards in their contracts with us and on the DPDP Act’s rules on transfers outside India.

6. How long we keep it

  • Business enquiries: for as long as we are in discussion or working together, and up to 3 years after our last contact.
  • Candidate profiles and resumes: up to 2 years after our last contact with you, so we can consider you for future roles, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Records we must keep by law are kept for the period the law requires.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • give you a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it (for example, remove your resume from our database);
  • withdraw a consent you have given;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.

To make a request, email info@talnxt.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We may need to confirm your identity before acting, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. How we protect it

Submissions travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and are stored in our Microsoft 365 environment, where access is limited to the TalNXT staff who need it. Our form-handling service can only write to the specific storage set up for this website. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a personal data breach as the law requires.

9. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics only if you accept analytics cookies in the banner shown on your first visit. If you decline, analytics is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time: . Cloudflare’s security check may use strictly necessary signals to tell people from bots; these are not used for advertising or tracking.

If we show LinkedIn posts on our pages, LinkedIn may set its own cookies when those posts load. LinkedIn’s privacy policy applies to that content.

10. Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Grievances and contact

If you have a question or complaint about how we handle your personal data, please contact our Grievance Officer at info@talnxt.com or call +91 96066 29269. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
